# Team Reports

Each team wrote this from its own seat, knowing the limits of the humans: Jacob Gruver (8 h/week, assumed), Mike Mears (4 h/week, assumed), Ben (1.5 h/week, assumed). Agents do the heavy lifting; humans approve, sign, pay, publish and play.

## Assumptions (confirm and edit in data-team.js)

- Jacob gives about 8 hours a week, Mike about 4, Ben about 1.5: 13.5 human hours a week in total.
- Cash budget is near zero until the first revenue: a hard cap of $100 a month, then raised only by Tithe's break-even sheets.
- Nobody on the team is a lawyer, accountant or engineer. Claude builds and drafts; humans sign, pay, publish and play.
- The art is made by Jacob in ChatGPT and stays that way. Agents never generate the final character art.
- Mike's exact role is open. The plan proposes: Jacob owns creative, Mike owns money and vendors, both sign off on launches.

## The humans

- **Jacob Gruver**, Founder and Executive Producer. Makes every portrait and background, owns canon and final approval, works with Claude on the site. *Limits:* Part-time. Art is the bottleneck: about 4 to 5 finished character pairs a week at best. Not a lawyer, accountant or engineer.
- **Mike Mears**, Partner (role to be set by you two). Proposed: owns accounts, money, vendors and outreach. Signs up for the services Claude sets up, and is the second human on every council decision. *Limits:* Part-time. Skills and hours not yet confirmed: the plan assumes no engineering or art load.
- **Ben**, Creative Consultant and Tester. Reviews art and story against the tone, plays every build and prototype, runs the playtests, reports bugs. *Limits:* A few hours a week, on request. Not an owner of deliverables.

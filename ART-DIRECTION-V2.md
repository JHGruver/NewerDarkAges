# Art Direction V2: Shard Cel

Flat, simple shapes painted calmly, drawn with spiky ink and stretched hero proportions, dressed in bold heroic costume.

Generated from `data-artdir2.js`. The same text drives the Art Direction page, the prompts and the spreadsheets. No artist or show is named anywhere: the look is described by its elements.

## Where the look comes from

**Family A: Flat-shape cinema** (decides how it is painted)
- Big flat color areas with nothing blended
- Very few lines, very simple shapes
- Calm negative space so the character is the loudest thing
- A confident silhouette first, detail second

**Family B: Scratch-ink action comics** (decides how it moves)
- Rubbery, stretched bodies with oversized hands and feet
- Jagged, spiky shapes in hair, cloth and weapons
- Ink lines that swell and taper to a point
- Heavy pure-black fills in the deepest corners
- Extreme perspective: the nearest hand or foot is huge
- Wild, readable faces with sharp teeth

**Family C: Manga-flavored hero fantasy** (decides how it is dressed)
- Tall heroic bodies with broad shoulders and a tapered waist
- Oversized boots, gauntlets, shoulder plates and capes
- Hair and cloth swept by the motion into big shapes
- Ornamental trim made of simple repeated shapes
- Large expressive eyes
- Big confident hero poses

**Blend rule.** A decides how it is painted. B decides how it moves. C decides how it is dressed. When two ideas disagree, A wins on color and rendering, B wins on shape and line, C wins on costume and pose.

## The twelve elements

### E1 Shape language

Everything is built from sharp wedges, triangles and slabs. Hair, cloth hems, capes, tails and feathers end in long points.

Prompt wording (never reword):

> Shape language: sharp angular shards, wedges and triangles. Hair, cloth hems, capes and tails end in long pointed blades. The silhouette reads instantly, even as a tiny thumbnail.

Do: One dominant shape per body part; Repeat the same shard angle across the figure; Check the silhouette in solid black.

Don't: Round soft blobs; Fussy curls; Silhouettes that need their detail to read.

### E2 Proportions

Small head, broad shoulders, tapered waist, long elastic limbs. Hands and feet are about one and a half times natural size. Bigger bodies get bulkier, not taller.

Prompt wording (never reword):

> Stylized heroic proportions: small head, broad shoulders, tapered waist, long elastic limbs, and hands and feet oversized to about one and a half times natural size.

Do: Push the hands and feet first; Keep the head small so the pose has room; Let the physique line from the character sheet override the default.

Don't: Realistic anatomy; Tiny hands; Chibi big heads.

### E3 Perspective push

In every pose the nearest hand, weapon or foot is thrust toward the viewer and drawn much larger.

Prompt wording (never reword):

> Exaggerated perspective: the nearest hand, weapon or foot is pushed toward the viewer and drawn much larger (foreshortening), with squash and stretch on the body.

Do: Pick one oversized limb per character; Let the body twist behind it.

Don't: Flat front-on poses; Two or three foreshortened limbs fighting each other.

### E4 Ink line

Thick black outer contour, medium interior lines, thin detail lines. Every line tapers to a point. The edge is a little rough and very confident.

Prompt wording (never reword):

> Ink line: thick black outer contour, medium interior lines, thin detail lines, with tapered pointed line endings and a slightly rough, confident edge. No sketchy hatching or cross-hatching.

Do: Heavier line on the underside of forms; Let lines end in a flick.

Don't: Hatching; Uniform wire-thin line; Double outlines.

### E5 Flat fill

Every area is one solid color. Nothing is blended, glowing, blurred or textured.

Prompt wording (never reword):

> Flat solid color fills only. No gradients, no soft airbrush, no texture, no painterly strokes.

Do: Large calm color areas; Let the empty areas rest.

Don't: Gradients; Glow; Noise or grain.

### E6 Two-tone shading

Each material has its base color and one darker hard-edged shadow shape. The deepest creases get solid pure black.

Prompt wording (never reword):

> Shading is two flat tones per material: the base color and one darker hard-edged shadow shape. Solid pure-black spot fills mark the deepest creases (under the chin, inside cloth folds, between the legs).

Do: Shadow shapes that echo the shard angle; Black spots under the chin, arms and cloth folds.

Don't: Three or more tones per material; Soft shadow edges; Gray shadows.

### E7 Face

Large angular eyes, a readable heavy brow, a wedge of a nose, a big mouth. Expression is the character's attitude in one look.

Prompt wording (never reword):

> Expressive face: large angular eyes, a heavy readable brow, a wedge nose and an exaggerated mouth. Sharp teeth are allowed.

Do: One clear emotion; Brows carry the mood; Faceless characters keep the head shape expressive.

Don't: Neutral blank stares (unless the sheet says blank); Realistic noses and lips.

### E8 Costume detail

Exactly three signature costume shapes, bold and chunky. Trim is a simple repeated shape. No tiny detail.

Prompt wording (never reword):

> Low detail: exactly three signature costume shapes, drawn bold and chunky. Trim is a simple repeated shape. No tiny details, no engraved texture.

Do: Name the three shapes in the outfit line; Oversize belts, buckles, boots and gauntlets.

Don't: Pockets, stitching, text, logos; More than about 70 words of outfit.

### E9 Materials

Each material has a drawing shortcut so the same thing always looks the same.

- **Metal:** One hard white highlight shape and one dark shadow shape
- **Cloth:** Three sharp fold triangles, hem in points
- **Fur, feathers, grass:** Chunky shard clusters, never single hairs
- **Skin:** Flat tone with one shadow shape and black under the chin
- **Stone, bone, shell:** Blocky planes with one crack line
- **Glass, glow, flame:** A flat bright shape with a darker outline, never blurred

### E10 Palette

Exactly three accent colors plus black, white and grey. About 60 percent main, 30 percent secondary, 10 percent pop. Outlines are near-black. The people color is reserved for the background.

Do: Use the three hex values from the character sheet exactly; Keep skin tones from the skin chart.

Don't: A fourth hue; The people color on the character; Pure grey shadows.

### E11 Pose and camera

A strong diagonal line of action. Weight on one leg. Slight low camera so the figure feels big. Full body, uncropped.

Do: S-curve or diagonal spine; Hair and cloth swept by the motion.

Don't: Straight vertical standing; Cropped hands, feet or weapons.

### E12 Background stage

Backgrounds use the same rules: flat shapes in three depth planes, sharp architecture, one flat moon, color bands for the sky. The people color washes about 65 percent. The character's trio shows through as about 20 percent accents.

Do: Black silhouette shapes at the edges; People-color mid shapes; Pale haze far shapes in the same color.

Don't: Stippling, grain or smudge; Blurred glow; Characters in the scene.

## Proportions by size

- **tiny:** tiny and cartoonishly squashed: head one quarter of the height, hands and feet very large
- **small:** small and wiry: head one fifth of the height, big hands and feet, springy limbs
- **medium:** medium heroic build: head one sixth of the height, long limbs, big hands and feet
- **large:** large and bulky: blocky torso, small head sunk between huge shoulders, fists and feet twice natural size
- **huge:** huge and massive: a mountain of shapes, tiny head or no clear head, enormous limbs

## Pose energy by type

- **tank:** a planted wide low stance, the shield or fist thrust toward the viewer and drawn huge, weight sunk into the legs, a chunky layered silhouette
- **bruiser:** caught mid-swing with the striking fist or weapon arcing toward the viewer and drawn huge, back leg driving, torso twisted
- **fighter:** a balanced dynamic stance caught mid-strike, one arm striking toward the viewer, the other guarding, a strong diagonal line of action
- **assassin:** a low predatory crouch or mid-leap, one blade hand thrust toward the viewer, the body stretched along a long diagonal, cloth trailing in sharp shards
- **caster:** one hand thrust toward the viewer casting and drawn huge, the other holding the focus back, clothes whipped into sharp points by the energy
- **blaster:** a braced aggressive firing or throwing stance, the weapon or throwing arm foreshortened toward the viewer, back arched, the muzzle or throw clearly readable
- **controller:** a wide sweeping arm gesture deploying a trap, chain or barrier toward the viewer, tools hanging off the body, torso twisted
- **support:** an open generous lunge with one hand reaching toward the viewer offering aid, the kit clearly visible, a warm confident expression
- **summoner:** arms flung wide or one hand commanding, small summoned allies bursting out around the figure drawn small and clearly separate, a strong S-curve body
- **enchanter:** raised hands presenting the talisman or instrument toward the viewer in a flourish, ornamental accessories catching the swing

## How every prompt is built

1 Name line: Name, type, role, people and class. One line.
2 Identity: Who they are, from the cast sheet. One sentence.
3 Body: Physique, size, movement, proportion recipe from the size chart.
4 Face: The expression from the cast sheet plus the face element.
5 Costume: The outfit line (70 words at most) plus the one signature exaggeration.
6 Pose: The pose from the cast sheet plus the pose energy for the type.
7 Style kit: Elements E1 to E8, word for word. Never reword.
8 Color: Three hex values and the 60/30/10 rule. The people color is banned from the figure.
9 Framing and output: Full body, 3:4, transparent PNG, mature, no text.
10 Not: The one-line negative list.

Framing: FRAMING (strict): FULL BODY, head to toe, slightly low camera. Every part of the figure is visible: the whole head, both hands, both feet or legs, any tail, wings or tentacles and the full length of every weapon. NOTHING is cropped at any edge. The figure fills about 85 percent of the height with a clear margin all around.

Wearables: WEARABLE AND HAND-HELD ONLY: show only what the character wears or holds in their hands. No vehicles, mounts, buildings, towers, machines, furniture or scenery. No ground, floor or shadow.

Output: 3:4 portrait PNG with genuine alpha transparency: no background, no checkerboard, no floor, no cast shadow, no text. Mature, adult tone. No nudity, no gore, no blood.

Negative: Not realistic, not photographic, not 3D rendered, not painterly, not watercolor, no halftone dots, no gradients, no glow, no lens flare, no particles.

## Style lock

> STYLE LOCK (paste when asking for changes): Keep the Shard Cel look exactly: sharp angular shards, stylized heroic proportions with oversized hands and feet, perspective push toward the viewer, thick-to-thin tapered ink line, flat solid fills, two-tone hard shadows with pure-black spot fills, three signature costume shapes only, same three accent colors, full body on transparent background.

## The Shard Cel test

1. The silhouette reads at thumbnail size
2. Every fill is flat, two tones at most, plus black spots
3. The outline is thick outside, thin inside, with pointed ends
4. The shapes are angular and hair and cloth end in points
5. One hand, foot or weapon is oversized toward the viewer
6. Exactly three accent colors plus black, white and grey
7. Only three signature costume shapes
8. Full body, uncropped, on transparency

## Backgrounds

Bold flat-shape 2D illustration, 16:9 landscape. Layered flat color shapes in three depth planes: near black silhouette shapes at the edges, mid shapes in the people color, far shapes in a pale haze of the same color. Sharp angular architecture built from wedges and slabs with hard-edged black shadow shapes. The sky is two or three flat color bands with one flat moon disc. Thin flat rim-light shapes on edges and flat glow discs behind lamps. No stippling, no grain, no smudge, no halftone, no gradients, no blur.

Keep the center-lower area open for the character. Props on the left, right and far plane only. No characters, creatures, figures or silhouettes of people or animals, no blood, and no text or lettering anywhere.

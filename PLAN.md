## Revenue streams and what unlocks them

| Stream | How it earns | Cost | Rough potential |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Supporter page | Monthly tiers: early reveals, wallpapers, vote power | Near zero | $100 to $500 a month at 25 to 100 supporters |
| Print-on-demand store | Prints, stickers, shirts, mugs from the cast art | Near zero, the vendor ships | $2 to $8 profit per item |
| Digital downloads | Wallpapers, character sheets, print-and-play cards | Near zero | Small, but it builds the list |
| Comic crowdfund | Pre-orders and tiers for issue #1 | Printing quote plus lettering | Low five figures if the list converts |
| Board game crowdfund | Pre-orders and stretch goals | Prototype and a manufacturer quote | Low to mid five figures for a first small box |
| Premium merch | Enamel pins, playmats, art books | Minimum runs of a few hundred | Margins of 40 to 60 percent |
| Licensing | Fees and royalties for tabletop RPG, apparel, animation | Legal time | Unpredictable, high upside |
| Online game | Cosmetics and a battle pass, never pay to win | Large: a team and servers | Only after proven demand |

Potential figures are assumptions, not forecasts.

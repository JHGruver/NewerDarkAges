# The Newer Dark Ages: Lore Bible

*Version 1.0 (draft for approval).* In 1986 the world found out the monsters were real. The war that followed ended the old world.

**Source tags:** `S` From your World Outline · `X` From your Characters sheet · `A` Added to bridge the sources with the game. Needs your approval

## 1. The world in one paragraph

In the Cold War year of 1986, Moscow went on every frequency and announced that vampires exist. The panic became a war that tore down the old world's infrastructure and stunted its technology for decades. Humans, vampires and werewolves now live behind cordons under a fragile treaty enforced by their Mystics, who meet every three months at the Temple of Imhotep. Power is inherited only by those who survive the trials of the Mummy Sorcerer Thoth-Ra in the Black Pyramid; the rest of the Mystics' secrets are sold in blood. Now the Elders of all three tribes are dead, the Chieftains are not who they seem, and a generation of children with powers is about to inherit a world that looks like the Dark Ages lit by neon.

## 2. Five pillars

1. **The Broadcast broke the world** `[S+A]`. The Last Broadcast is a Cold War event, not a magic one: a government told the truth on every frequency and the world could not live with it. That is why radios, static and signals are sacred and dangerous in the Dim.
2. **Three tribes, one treaty** `[S]`. Humans, vampires and werewolves each rule themselves, never cross each other's borders, and answer to the same laws. Peace is the only thing keeping the old war from restarting.
3. **Power has a price and a pyramid** `[S]`. Real power (magic) comes from one place: the trials of Thoth-Ra in the Black Pyramid. Pass and you become a Mystic. Fail and your soul is forfeit.
4. **Justice is a one-way road** `[S]`. Everyone is tried by the same law. The worst are sent to the Block and escorted through the Zombie Territories by Dr. Stein's modified zombies. Nobody comes back.
5. **The old are dying, the young are waking** `[X+A]`. All nine Elders are dead. The Chieftains hold the chairs. The people who will decide what happens next are between 6 and 28 years old and each carries a power.

## 3. Timeline

- **Before: The Quiet World** `[S+A]`. Humans, vampires and werewolves already shared the planet, hidden from each other. Older still: Egyptian gods, a Black Pyramid in the south, and a Mummy Sorcerer who was waiting.
- **1986: The Last Broadcast** `[S+A]`. At the height of the Cold War, Russia announced the existence of vampires on every frequency. Sixty seconds of transmission, then nothing but static. The world could not take it back.
- **1986 to the 1990s: The War to End the Old World** `[S]`. The announcement became a full-fledged war across the world. Infrastructure was torn apart. Technology was stunted for the years that followed.
- **After the war: The Dim** `[S+A]`. The lights did not come back. Rain, fog, dead radio towers and a world that reverted to keeps, oaths and torches, with the old world's relics (cassettes, CRT screens, batteries) as treasure.
- **After the war: The Cordon** `[S]`. Humans, vampires and werewolves each cordoned off their own territory and agreed to strict rules of peace so that each tribe could rebuild.
- **Every 3 months: The Mystic Meetings** `[S]`. The Mystics of each colony travel to the Temple of Imhotep, god of wisdom, medicine and magic, to air grievances and renew the treaty.
- **Ongoing: The Trials** `[S]`. Each tribe's Senate, Counsel or Pack Leads choose who may rise, but the Mystics pick the one to ascend. The chosen must reach the Black Pyramid to the south and pass the trials of the Mummy Sorcerer Thoth-Ra. Those who fail are sacrificed and their souls are forfeit.
- **After the cordon: Dr. Stein's Sentries** `[S]`. Human scientist Franklin Stein captured zombies and implanted behavior-modification chips. Zombies do not attack their own, so modified zombies masked the colony's scent and became sentries. The Vampire Lords were impressed. The Werewolf Greys did not like zombies at their borders, but agreed to use them as prisoner escorts.
- **After the cordon: The Block** `[S]`. A large prison in neutral territory. Prisoners from all three colonies go there and there is no chance of release. Escorts walk them through the Zombie Territories.
- **Later: The Upgrades** `[S]`. To ferry prisoners, Stein built cybernetic zombies: stronger, armored, able to take basic commands and run contingency plans for escapes. Not mindless any more, but still docile.
- **Later: The Temple of Ramuset** `[S]`. Just north of the Block, on a small hill, stands a temple to the god Ramuset, left over from the old world.
- **Recent: The Elders Die** `[X]`. All nine Elders (three per tribe) are dead, including Dante, Magnus, Sullivan, Cessily, Gabriel, Salina, Frederick, Lucas and Miriam. Sullivan shared secrets with Ezekiel before he died. Dr. Stein is dead too.
- **Now: The Heirs** `[X+A]`. Chieftain Ezekiel (an agent of the god Ramuset), Demetrius and Erik hold the chairs. Their children and grandchildren, aged 6 to 28, are waking with powers just as the next treaty renewal approaches.

## 4. Places

- **The Dim (Human Colony)** `[S+A]` (Human). The central wasteland where the old highways cross: radio masts, containers and torch-lit gates. The human colony, ruled by a Senate and a Chieftain, advised by Elders.
- **The Static Spires (Vampire Colony)** `[S+A]` (Vampire). Skyscraper husks turned into courts. The vampire colony, ruled by a Counsel and a High Priest, advised by Lords.
- **The Pylon Coast and the Scrapwild (Werewolf Colony)** `[S+A]` (Were-Creature). Dead power lines strung like cathedral rigging, tire-wall war-dens and drowned vaults. The Pack, led by Pack Leaders and an Alpha Wolf, advised by Greys. The other were-kin clans (raccoon, crocodile, capybara and more) shelter under the Pack's banner.
- **The Zombie Territories** `[S+A]` (Undead). The wild lands between the colonies and the Block, overrun by zombies. Nobody crosses without an escort. Forest spirits call the greener edge of it the Overgrowth.
- **The Block** `[S]` (Undead). A large prison in neutral territory for the prisoners of all three colonies. Guarded by Stein's modified zombies. No release.
- **Temple of Ramuset** `[S]` (Magic). A temple to the god Ramuset on a small hill just north of the Block. Standing since the old world.
- **Temple of Imhotep** `[S+A]` (Magic). Neutral ground where the Mystics of all three tribes meet every three months to renew the treaty.
- **The Black Pyramid** `[S+A]` (Undead). To the south. Home of the Mummy Sorcerer Thoth-Ra and his trials. The only place a Chieftain, High Priest or Alpha can be made. Ashfall Reach is the dust and tombs that surround it.
- **The Drowned Undercity** `[A]` (Eldritch). Under the Static Spires: flooded subways and a drowned cathedral where the war's flood and the Broadcast's echo let the unmoored things come up. Outside the treaty.
- **The Dead Air Stations** `[A]` (Ghost). Dead broadcast stations and haunted highways, scattered over every territory. The war dead cling to old electronics. Neutral by default.

### Moving up the ranks

1. **Voted in** `[S]`. The colony votes members into the Senate, Counsel or Pack Leads. They run the colony and report to the head.
2. **Chosen** `[S]`. The Mystics choose one of the council to ascend to Chieftain, High Priest or Alpha Wolf.
3. **The Black Pyramid** `[S]`. The chosen travels south to the Black Pyramid and attempts the trials of the Mummy Sorcerer Thoth-Ra.
4. **Pass: a limited gift** `[S]`. They receive limited magical abilities, return home and learn the mystic arts from the Elders, Lords or Greys.
5. **Fail: forfeit** `[S]`. They are sacrificed to the Mummy and their souls are forfeit.

## 6. Reconciling the sources

- **The Last Broadcast** `[S+A]`. Becomes the Russian announcement of 1986, sent on every frequency. The sixty seconds of sound that followed is a design addition and is the root of the Second Signal.
- **Thoth-Ra, Imhotep and Ramuset** `[A]`. Three names in the sources. Proposed: Imhotep is the neutral god the Mystics meet under. Ramuset is a darker god who is worshipped north of the Block and whose agent is Ezekiel. Thoth-Ra is Ramuset's sorcerer, the Mummy at the Black Pyramid who runs the trials. This makes every ascended leader a possible agent of Ramuset.
- **The game's eight peoples** `[A]`. Humans, Vampires and Werewolves are the three tribes. Zombies, Zombie-Hybrids and the Mummy are the Undead. The Ghosts, Forest Spirits and Eldritch are the old world's leftovers and the things the war woke up. Magic is the Mystic tradition made into four classes.
- **Werewolves versus the Were-Creature people** `[A]`. The Pack (wolf-kin) is the werewolf colony. The other were-kin (raccoon, crocodile, capybara, squirrel, axolotl, raven, owl, cephalopod (octopus, squid, cuttlefish), mantis shrimp, moth, pangolin, spider) are clans that swore to the Pack in the cordon years.
- **Where magic comes from** `[A]`. From the Black Pyramid. The Wizards study what the trials teach, the Witches grow it, the Warlocks bargain for it (many through failed trials), and the Mages are born carrying it.

## 7. How the game's peoples, classes and subclasses fit

### Human · The Dim

The human colony. Chieftain, Senate and Elders. Nothing innate, so they win on nerve, wheels and salvage.

- **Roadwar Convoys** (Interceptors, Outriders, Fuel Zealots): The convoys that connect the colony to the Block, the Temple of Imhotep and the south road. Their drivers escort Mystics.
- **Arcade Clans** (Joystick Duelists, Pinball Gunners, Token Brokers): The colony's training clans. Competition is how the Senate finds talent.
- **Pirate Radio** (Static Jammers, Skyline DJs, Late-Night Hosts): The colony's voice. They are the only ones who still remember the exact words of the Broadcast.
- **Mall Republic** (Riot Marshals, Escalator Lancers, Concierge Diplomats): The seat of the Senate and the colony's police and envoys.
- **Summoners** (Ninja Shoguns): The Shogun houses: hereditary war-lords who keep a ninja clan on call. The Senate pretends not to know them and borrows them often.

### Vampire · The Static Spires

The vampire colony. High Priest, Counsel and Lords. Rule by aristocracy and style.

- **Chrome Court** (Razor Dandies, Mirror-Ball Executioners, Velvet Chaperones): The Counsel's households and their duelists. The nobility.
- **Neon Nocturnes** (Strobe Duelists, Bassline Brawlers, VIP Hosts): The night clubs that double as barracks and information markets.
- **Night Watch** (Owlwing Watch, Gargoyle Sentries, Antenna Wraiths): Rooftop guards who patrol the borders and enforce the treaty line.
- **Coterie of Masks** (Masked Assassins, Thorn Poisoners, Spotlight Understudies): The High Priest's spies and quiet knives.
- **Summoners** (Belfry Batcallers): Belfry colonies tended by Batcallers. The bats are the High Priest's eyes across the Spires, and the first to know when a treaty line is crossed.

The werewolf colony and the clans that swore to the Pack. Alpha Wolf, Pack Leaders and Greys.

- **Mantis Shrimp-kin** (Punch-Claw Duelists, Prism Gunners, Reef Shell-Guard): Reef guards and shock troops.
- **Moth-kin** (Lantern Cultists, Dreamdust Weavers, Signal-Tower Wardens): Light-keepers who tend the old signal towers.

### Ghost · The Dead Air Stations

The war dead who stayed in the machines. Neutral, and the only ones who remember the last day.

- **Dead Air** (Static Wraiths, Test-Pattern Seers, Emergency Broadcasters): Haunters of dead broadcast stations: the Broadcast's own echo.
- **Hollow Machines** (Rust-Wraith Possessors, Cabinet Spirits, Circuit Seers): Spirits bound into old hardware.

### Undead · The Block, the Zombie Territories and the Black Pyramid

Zombies, Dr. Stein's cybernetic zombie-hybrids, and the Mummy court of Thoth-Ra and Ramuset.

- **Dead Zone Legion** (Riot-Line Bonebreakers, Rattle-Cannon Gunners, Drum-Major Stewards): The Block's guards: Dr. Stein's modified, armored zombies. The line never breaks.

The old folk of the green edge of the Zombie Territories. They kept to themselves when the world burned.

Not from here. The flood and the Broadcast's echo let the unmoored things up under the Static Spires. Outside the treaty.

- **Deep Ones** (Tidecall Priests, Coralbone Sentinels, Pearl-Eyed Oracles): The drowned civilization under the Spires.

### Magic · Every colony, and the Black Pyramid

The Mystic tradition. Four ways to hold the power that Thoth-Ra grants and the power some are simply born with.

- **Wizard** (Signal Savants, Circuit Arcanists, Sticky Wizards): The academic Mystics: Elders and Lords who study what the trials teach.
- **Witch** (Spore Coven, Scraphex Witches, Thornblood Coven): The naturalist Mystics: Greys and wild practitioners who cultivate it.
- **Warlock** (Voidbound, Bloodwire Apostates, Mirror Oathbreakers): Those who bargained: the failed, the bound and the oathbreakers.
- **Mage** (Chronofractures, Mindweavers, Fluxborn): The born conduits: the generation now waking with powers.
- **Summoners** (Necromancers): The Mystic tradition's quiet fifth path: students of the Black Pyramid's sands who raise the old dead. The Mystics do not forbid it. They watch. (Proposed, tagged A.)

## 10. The story as a broadcast

1. **Signal lost: Sixty seconds. Every frequency.** 
2. **The Last Broadcast: They are real.** At the height of the Cold War, Moscow cut into every radio and television on Earth with one sentence: vampires exist. The world had sixty seconds to believe it.
3. **The war to end the old world: Panic became war.** The war took the bridges, the grid and the satellites. When it ended, nobody had the tools to build them back. Technology stalled for decades.
4. **The Dim: The lights stayed off.** Keeps, torches and oaths came back. A battery became treasure, a cassette became scripture, and the rain never quite stopped.
5. **Three tribes, one treaty: Nobody crosses a border.** Humans, vampires and werewolves took their own territories and swore to a strict peace. Only a Mystic may cross, and every three months the Mystics meet at the Temple of Imhotep to renew it.
6. **The three chairs: Chieftain. High Priest. Alpha.** Each tribe is run by a council, and led by one chosen by the Mystics. They only rise if they survive what waits in the south.
7. **The Black Pyramid: Pass, and you are given magic.** The trials of the Mummy Sorcerer Thoth-Ra decide who may lead. Pass and you receive a gift. Fail and your soul is forfeit.
8. **The Block: No one walks back.** The worst of every tribe go to one prison. The road runs through the Zombie Territories, and the escorts are Dr. Stein's zombies: armored, chipped and obedient.
9. **Nine empty chairs: The Elders are dead.** All nine. One of them shared his secrets with a Chieftain who answers to a god. Then there was nobody left to ask.
10. **Everything else woke up: The old things are listening.** Ghosts in the dead stations. Spirits in the Overgrowth. Things from under the flooded Spires. They answer a signal the tribes pretend not to hear.
11. **The heirs: The world is theirs to inherit or burn.** Anna feels what is about to happen. Josh carries the lightning. Tom sees through walls. Emma is six and she flies. They are 6 to 28 years old.
12. **Your frequency: Which signal do you answer?** Pick a people. Pick a class. Pick a character. The dial is yours.

## Appendix A. World Outline (verbatim)

### Overview

Character Classes: Humans, Vampires, Werewolves, Zombies, Zombie-Hybrids, Mummy

Human Chain of Command: Elders/Mystics- Chieftain- Senate Members

Vampire Chain of Command: Lords/Mystics- High Priest- Counsel Members

### World History

The world as we know it remained the same, until the Cold War, when Russia announced the existence of vampires, and the world went into full-fledged war, tearing apart the worldwide infrastructure, and leaving technology stunted for the years that followed. Humans, vampires, and werewolves have all cordoned off their own territories, and adhere to strict rules of peace in order for each tribe to build themselves back up from the chaos that the worlds dealt out to all who took part in the war to end the old world.

### Moving Up the Ranks

The colony will vote members into the position of Senate, Counsel, or Pack Lead, and there, the group runs the colony and reports to the Chieftain, High Priest, or Alpha Wolf. The Chieftain, High Priest, or Alpha Wolf is a member of the Senate, Counsel, or Pack Leads that is chosen by the Mystics to ascend to their ranks. The chosen one will only move up in the ranks if they can successfully travel to the Black Pyramid to the South and complete the trials of the Mummy Sorcerer, Thoth-Ra. Once the chosen one passes the trials, they are given limited magical abilities, and may return to their colony and learn the mystic arts from the Elders, Lords, or Grays. If they do not pass the trials, they are sacrificed to the Mummy and their souls become forfeit.

### Mystic Meetings

Every three months, the Mystics of each colony travel to the Temple of Imhotep, The Egyptian God of wisdom, medicine and magic where they converse and address any concerns that any one colony may have with the others and renew the peace treaty.

### Treaty Agreement

No member of any tribe is permitted to enter another tribe’s territory. In emergency situations, only ones accompanied by a Mystic can do so, but they must follow protocol and call for a meeting with that Tribe’s Mystics upon arrival.

### Judicial System

All colonies run by the same set of laws. Each colony’s Senate, Counsel, or Pack Leads will but the accused on trial, and they will either be sentenced to some kind of Public work program, or if the crime is severe, be sent to The Block, a large prison in neutral territory. Prisoners from al three colonies are sent to the prison and once imprisoned; there is no chance for release. Once the sentence is passed, the Mystics call for an escort from the prison and the guards escort the prisoner through the wilds of the “Zombie Territories.”

### Prison Guards

Once the colonies were secured, a human scientist by the name of Franklin Stein captured a handful of zombies and began experimenting on their brains. Knowing that they only operate on the most basic of human instincts, he created behavior modification chips that he could use to control the zombies. With the implants in place, he stationed a few of his subjects around the Human territory to act as sentries. Because zombies do not attack their own kind, the modified zombies’ scents masked the human’s inside the colony’s borders. This was a great advancement in security, and once he had perfected the chips, the elders granted him an audience with the Vampire Lords, who were also very impressed. The Grays of the Werewolf tribe however, did not find comfort in having zombies stationed right outside their borders, controlled or not. They still agreed to use the monsters to transport prisoners, so that they would not have to brave the wilds of the “Zombie Territory.”

When the need arose for the modified zombies to ferry prisoners to the prison, Dr. Stein went back to work, creating enhancements for the zombies. He made cybernetic upgrades to increase their strength and gave them body armor, so as not to be as easily killed. He also gave them behavior upgrades, making them more free-thinking and able to understand basic commands. The behavior upgrades also came in handy if a prisoner ever tried to escape. They were programmed with a series of contingencies to apprehend prisoners that escaped their grasp and to deal with a multitude of other problems that may occur. They were still very much the sentries that Dr. Stein created at the beginning, but now they were so much more than mindless zombies. They relied more on sense than instinct now, but still were docile enough to not be considered a threat.

### The Temple of Ramuset

Just north of the prison, on a small hill, there is a temple for the Egyptian God Ramuset. Though it was built in his name, in this new world, It still stands from the old world.
